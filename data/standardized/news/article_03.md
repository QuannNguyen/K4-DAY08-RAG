# Dormitory rules updated for safety

The residence office reminded students to follow updated dormitory safety rules. All residents must register visitors before entry and keep emergency exits clear at all times. Residents are expected to maintain clean shared spaces and report maintenance issues quickly. Fire drills will occur at the beginning of each semester.
