# Scholarship application window opens

The scholarship office opened applications for merit-based support for undergraduate and graduate students. Applicants must submit transcripts, a statement of purpose, and recommendation letters before the deadline. Selection is based on academic excellence, leadership, and demonstration of financial need. Students already receiving partial support may apply for additional funds if they maintain academic standing. The review committee will publish decisions within eight weeks. # Scholarship application window opens
